Cybersecurity & Intel Club, Roger Williams University

A student community for cybersecurity and information security — built around meetings, guest speakers, and competition.

What we do

  • Weekly meetings covering security tools, concepts, and current events
  • Guest talks from industry and faculty speakers
  • Team entries in the Northeast Collegiate Cyber Defense Competition
  • Trips to professional conferences: DEF CON, BSides, Hackfest
