About

The Cybersecurity and Intel Club (CIC) meets weekly to talk about cyber news, run hands-on demonstrations, and travel to professional conferences.

As a club, we focus on many broad categories of cybersecurity, like red-team/blue-team exercises, forensics, system administration, as well as intelligence, including OSINT and threat analysis.

We encourage students of all majors and skill levels to participate, whether committing to the repository, attending meetings, or just following along with the demos.

What we do

  • Hands-on demosBi-weekly: Linux basics, forensics, and more
